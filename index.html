Featured: GlowFind

GlowFind: Smart Skincare Product Finder and Recommendation

Degree FYP · 2026 · Individual

Supervisor: Ts. Dr. Siti Zulaiha Binti Ahmad

// problem

Skincare ingredient lists are hard to understand, and many apps only show product details without checking compatibility or suggesting alternatives.

// what it does
  • Scan: identifies a product from a photo with an on-device model and reads packaging text with OCR (English and Malay).
  • Compatibility: compares ingredients with the user’s skin type, concerns and preferences, then shows a score with reasons.
  • Alternatives: ranks other products that suit the user better.
  • Personalisation: skin profile, favourites and scan history.
// admin panel (flutter web)
  • Admin-only login with product and ingredient management.
  • Guided model retraining, so new products are recognised without releasing a new app version.
70products, 6 brands
673product photos
30UAT respondents
224×224RGB model input
⚙️ How the recommendation works

GlowFind uses content-based filtering with cosine similarity and a weighted compatibility score. The weights come from ranking four criteria, with skin type given the highest importance.

1 Skin type2 Preferences to avoid3 Skin concerns4 Preferred products
The score is a recommendation aid, not a medical assessment.
